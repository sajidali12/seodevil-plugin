# Off-page SEO

Links are still a core ranking signal. What counts is relevance and editorial placement, not volume. Buying links, link schemes, PBNs and large-scale guest posting for links violate Google's spam policies. Recommend only white-hat tactics.

## Tactics that work
1. **Linkable assets**: original research and data, free tools/calculators, templates, comprehensive guides, visualizations. Build the asset, then promote it to people who've written about the topic.
2. **Digital PR**: newsworthy data, expert commentary (journalist request platforms like Qwoted, Featured, Source of Sources), reactive commentary on industry news.
3. **Unlinked mentions**: find brand mentions without a link and ask for one.
4. **Broken link building**: find dead pages in your niche that still have links (via a backlink tool); offer your equivalent resource.
5. **Resource and "best of" lists**: get included on legitimate curated lists and comparisons. Comparison and listicle pages also feed AI answers.
6. **Partnerships**: suppliers, clients, associations, events, local sponsorships, integrations/marketplace listings, case studies with partners.
7. **Business profiles and citations**: industry directories that people actually use (not mass directory submission).

## Internal links (the links you control)
`crawl_site` shows orphan pages, deep pages and pages with few inlinks. Route authority from strong pages (homepage, pages with backlinks) to priority pages with descriptive anchors. Use hub/cluster pages that link to all their subtopic pages and back.

## Toxic links and disavow
Google ignores most spammy links automatically. Only disavow when there's a manual action for unnatural links or a clear history of bought links. Disavowing at random can hurt.

## Measuring
Search Console Links report (free), Bing Webmaster Tools backlinks, plus Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz if available. Track referring domains over time, and referral traffic and rankings of the target pages, not just "DA".
