# Content SEO

## What Google rewards
Content written for people that satisfies the query better than what already ranks: first-hand experience, original information, clear expertise, a trustworthy site. Scaled thin content (AI-generated or not), keyword-stuffed pages and "SEO content" written to a word count are what core updates demote.

## On-page checklist
- **Title** (50-60 chars, check with `serp_preview`): primary keyword early, specific benefit, matches intent. Unique per page.
- **Meta description** (120-155 chars): summarizes the answer/offer and earns the click. Google rewrites it often, but a good one still wins on many queries.
- **URL**: short, lowercase, hyphens, the keyword, no dates unless news.
- **H1**: one clear statement of the topic, close to the title.
- **Intro**: answer the query or promise the outcome in the first 2 sentences. No throat-clearing.
- **Headings**: a logical H2/H3 outline that someone could skim. Use question headings where people ask questions.
- **Body**: cover the subtopics competitors cover (`compare_pages` gaps) plus something they don't (information gain: data, examples, screenshots, templates, expert quotes, an opinion from experience).
- **Media**: original images with descriptive alt text and file names; tables for comparisons; video where useful.
- **Internal links**: from relevant pages *to* this one with descriptive anchors, and from this one to deeper pages. New pages need at least 3 internal links in.
- **Structured data**: Article/BlogPosting with author and dates; Product/FAQ/HowTo-style content where it fits (`schema-markup` skill).
- **Freshness**: update facts, dates and screenshots, and say what changed. Don't just bump the date.

## E-E-A-T signals you can add
Named author with a bio and credentials, author page, about page, contact details, sources and citations, first-hand photos/tests, reviews from real customers, editorial policy (YMYL topics: health, money, legal, safety need this most).

## Briefs
Intent + reader's goal, title/description/slug/H1, full outline, questions with direct answers, terms/entities to cover, information-gain angle, target length range taken from the competitor median, internal links, schema, media, E-E-A-T elements.

## Content refresh (often the best ROI)
Find pages with declining clicks or positions 5-20 (Search Console), compare with the current top results (`compare_pages`), fill the gaps, improve the title for CTR, update facts, add internal links, re-request indexing.

## Cannibalization
Two pages targeting the same intent split signals. Merge into the stronger URL and 301 the other, or differentiate their intents clearly.

## Readability
`analyze_content` gives Flesch scores (English only). Target plain language for general audiences: short sentences, active voice, short paragraphs, lists and tables for scannable information.
